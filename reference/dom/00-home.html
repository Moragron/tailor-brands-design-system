FAQ

What do I need to start an LLC?

To form your LLC, you’ll need your business name, the state you’re registering in, a physical business address, and the names of any members or owners. You’ll also need a registered agent, which we can provide.

Can Tailor help me stay compliant?

Yes. Tailor offers compliance tools that track deadlines, file your annual reports, and handle state renewals for you. You’ll get reminders and support, so nothing slips through the cracks.

That way, you stay compliant without the hassle or risk of penalties.

How does the AI assistant (Navi) actually help?

Navi is the AI-powered assistant that guides you through your business journey. It tracks your progress, sends personalized reminders, and recommends next steps based on your stage and goals.
Navi helps you:

  • Know what to do next and why
  • Stay on top of filings and tax deadlines
  • Track invoices, forms, and application statuses
  • Keep everything organized behind the scenes
What if I’ve never started a business before?

That’s exactly who we’re built for. Our platform walks you through every step clearly, even if you’ve never done this before.

What makes Tailor different from other services?

We go beyond formation. With AI-powered guidance, compliance tools, and brand-building features, you get everything you need in one place, made for people figuring it out as they go.

Can I talk to someone if I get stuck?

Absolutely, we’re here to support you. You can reach our support team using the chat or call us at 1-332-286-2770.
